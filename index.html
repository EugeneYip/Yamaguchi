STRUCTURAL LIMITS OF NEW-PRODUCT FORECASTING

0:30-1:30 60 sec 102 words 102 wpm
ENGLISH SCRIPT · ACTUAL SPEAKING VERSION
MEMORY PATH · GLANCE HERE FIRST
2–3×→TWO PROBLEMS→DRIVERS→NUMERICAL IMPACT→MANAGEMENT PROBLEM
KEY NUMBER

Yamaguchi reports that weighted MAPE for new products is roughly two to three times the level for existing products.

MEANING

And since weighted MAPE is an error measure, what it means is that new products have worse forecast performance.

PROBLEM 1

He identifies two structural problems. First, the company must decide which changing factors actually drive demand. Those relationships could be affected by consumer, channel, promotion, and market conditions.

PROBLEM 2

Second, it must estimate each driver’s numerical impact with very little product-specific evidence.

SO WHAT

The calculated range may become too wide or may conflict with practitioner intuition. This structural limitation then creates a measurement and management problem.

Cue labels and the MEMORY PATH are rehearsal aids only — they are not spoken.
中文備稿

Yamaguchi 指出，新產品的加權平均絕對百分比誤差（weighted MAPE）大約是既有產品的二至三倍。因為 weighted MAPE 本身是誤差衡量指標，所以數值越高，代表預測表現越差。他認為新產品預測有兩個結構性問題。第一，公司必須判斷哪些變動因素真正會驅動需求，例如消費者特性、銷售通路、促銷活動與市場環境。第二，公司必須在缺乏產品本身歷史資料的情況下，估計每一項需求驅動因素的數值影響。這些估計的範圍可能過寬，也可能與實務人員的判斷互相衝突，因此問題不只是統計模型本身，也會進一步變成衡量與管理上的問題。

Q&A DEFENSE

If asked about the wording, say: The article calls it forecast accuracy, but weighted-MAPE is an error metric. I preserve the reported 2-3x level and explain the implication separately.

[Sources]
- Yamaguchi (2024), pp. 9-10.

MEASUREMENT DISCIPLINE AND THE PRESERVED BASELINE

1:30-2:35 65 sec 110 words 102 wpm
ENGLISH SCRIPT · ACTUAL SPEAKING VERSION
MEMORY PATH · GLANCE HERE FIRST
MEASUREMENT DISCIPLINE→TWO JOBS→AI VALUE→BASELINE ≠ TARGET→ACTION
MAIN POINT

The article’s sharper point is measurement discipline.

SETUP

A preserved base forecast does two jobs.

JOB 1

First, it lets a company compare forecast versions with actuals, calculate weighted MAPE, Bias, or FVA, and isolate whether AI added value.

EVIDENCE

Yamaguchi notes that fewer than half of his surveyed companies regularly evaluate accuracy, and actuals may be stored while forecasts are not.

JOB 2

Second, the same base forecast must stay independent of management’s target.

GAP → ACTION

The gap between evidence and ambition becomes the reason for stakeholder discussion and demand-shaping action.

TAKEAWAY

The baseline should not be rewritten to match the target. So it’s more about a different organizational use of forecasting, not just about a better model.

Cue labels and the MEMORY PATH are rehearsal aids only — they are not spoken.
中文備稿

這篇文章更尖銳的重點其實是「衡量紀律」。保留下來的基準預測有兩個作用。第一，公司可以把不同版本的預測與實際結果相比較，計算加權 MAPE、偏誤（Bias）或預測附加價值（FVA），並進一步判斷人工智慧是否真的帶來改善。Yamaguchi 指出，在他的受訪公司中，不到一半會定期評估預測準確度，而且企業可能有保存實際值，卻沒有保存當時的預測版本。第二，同一個基準預測必須與管理目標保持獨立。客觀證據與管理企圖之間的差距，才應成為利害關係人討論與需求塑造行動的起點。換句話說，不能為了讓數字看起來符合目標，就回頭改寫基準預測；真正的重點是改變組織如何使用預測，而不只是追求一個更好的模型。

SENSE-MAKING DEMAND FORECASTING

FIGURE

This is the only figure in the article. Yamaguchi applies Karl Weick’s sense-making idea.

SENSE-MAKING

Under uncertainty, practitioners act, try to explain what happened, and adjust instead of waiting for perfect information.

TOP

This figure shows how forecasting supports that process. The upper part moves from demand forecasting to stakeholder consensus, action, and business growth.

BOTTOM

The lower half compares traditional attention to algorithms, data engineering, and short-term accuracy with transparent causal assumptions and coordinated action.

WHY ACCURACY STILL MATTERS

Accuracy still matters because it improves service levels and inventory turns.

STARTING POINT

But the forecast is now a disciplined starting point.

CROSS-FUNCTIONAL

Sales, Marketing, Finance, and Supply Chain compare the objective baseline with the target, expose assumptions, and choose demand-shaping levers.

BRIDGE

This learning then needs to become reusable, which leads to the demand library.

中文備稿

這是文章中唯一的一張圖。Yamaguchi 在這裡運用 Karl Weick 的「意義建構」概念：當環境高度不確定時，實務人員不可能等到所有資訊都完整才行動，而是先採取行動、觀察結果、解釋發生了甚麼，再持續調整。圖的上半部呈現從需求預測、形成利害關係人共識、決定行動，到最後帶動企業成長的流程；下半部則比較兩種不同重心：傳統預測比較重視演算法、資料工程與短期準確度，而意義建構式預測更強調透明的因果假設、跨部門協調，以及如何把預測轉化成行動。準確度仍然重要，因為它會影響服務水準與存貨週轉率；但在這個架構下，預測更像是一個有紀律的共同起點。業務、行銷、財務與供應鏈可以拿客觀基準與目標比較，公開彼此的假設，再決定要使用哪些需求塑造手段。這些經驗如果要持續累積，就必須變成可重複使用的知識，也因此引出下一頁的需求知識庫。

If asked whether this is just S&OP, answer: It shares cross-functional consensus, but Yamaguchi emphasizes a transparent base forecast and causal drivers that trigger demand-shaping action for new products. Sense-making comes from Karl Weick's 2005 idea of acting, interpreting, and adjusting under uncertainty.

DEMAND LIBRARY

3:55-4:55 60 sec 93 words 93 wpm
ENGLISH SCRIPT · ACTUAL SPEAKING VERSION
MEMORY PATH · GLANCE HERE FIRST
REUSABLE LEARNING→4 FUNCTIONS→HIDDEN INFORMATION→VISIBLE DRIVERS→NEXT LAUNCH
PURPOSE

To make that learning reusable, Yamaguchi proposes a demand library.

DEFINITION

Yamaguchi defines a demand library as a mechanism that gathers, connects, internalizes, and externalizes tacit knowledge.

WHAT GOES IN

New-product forecasting depends on information that may not appear clearly in a dataset. These could be product attributes, market context, launch conditions, promotion quality, and practitioner judgment.

VALUE

The library makes drivers and assumptions visible, so every launch strengthens the next one instead of starting from zero every time.

BUSINESS USE

It also helps Marketing and Product Managers understand which levers may increase demand and combine forecasting with demand shaping.

Cue labels and the MEMORY PATH are rehearsal aids only — they are not spoken.
中文備稿

為了讓每一次上市累積的經驗能被下一次重複使用，Yamaguchi 提出「需求知識庫（demand library）」的概念。他把它定義成一套可以蒐集、連結、內化與外化內隱知識的機制。新產品預測會依賴很多不一定清楚存在於資料集中的資訊，例如產品特性、市場情境、上市條件、促銷品質，以及實務人員的判斷。需求知識庫的功能，就是把這些需求驅動因素與假設明確留下來，讓每一次上市都能強化下一次，而不是每次重新從零開始。它也能協助行銷與產品經理更清楚知道哪些手段可能提高需求，進而把「預測需求」和「主動塑造需求」連接在一起。

Q&A DEFENSE

If asked for the definition, use the author's four functions exactly: a mechanism that gathers, connects, internalizes, and externalizes tacit knowledge through iteration and continuous learning.

[Sources]
- Yamaguchi (2024), p. 12. The process layout is the presenter's explanatory synthesis; the four functions follow the article.

01What is the main argument of the article?
For new products, forecasting should stay as an objective baseline, then help the organization see the target gap and decide what action to take.
IF PRESSED The forecast is not the target. The gap between them is what triggers discussion and demand-shaping action.
02Why are new products harder to forecast?
Because they have very little product-specific history, so it is harder to identify the right demand drivers and estimate their numerical impact.
IF PRESSED That is why new-product forecast error can be much higher than for existing products.
03What does the 2–3× weighted MAPE mean?
Weighted MAPE is an error measure, so a higher number means worse forecast performance. Yamaguchi reports the new-product level at roughly two to three times the existing-product level.
IF PRESSED I would treat the 2–3× figure as a practitioner observation because the article does not provide a full sample or research design.
04What is the difference between a forecast and a target?
A forecast estimates what is likely to happen under stated assumptions. A target is what management wants to happen.
IF PRESSED Yamaguchi’s point is that we should not rewrite the baseline just to make it equal the target.
05What is sense-making demand forecasting?
It uses the forecast as a transparent starting point so different functions can understand the situation, discuss the gap, and decide actions.
IF PRESSED Yamaguchi applies Karl Weick’s sense-making idea: under uncertainty, people act, interpret what happens, and adjust.
06What is a demand library?
It is a mechanism for gathering, connecting, internalizing, and externalizing tacit knowledge so one product launch can strengthen the next one.
IF PRESSED It makes drivers and assumptions visible. The article gives the concept, not a detailed database design.
07Why is accuracy still important if the article focuses on action?
Accuracy still matters because better forecasts can improve service levels and inventory turns. The point is that accuracy is not the only business objective.
IF PRESSED The forecast also needs to support decisions, learning, and demand shaping.
08What is your answer to the final question about judging the original forecast?
Preserve the baseline, document the interventions, and evaluate forecast error separately from decision impact.
IF PRESSED I present this as my current answer. The article does not fully specify how that measurement system should be designed.

09Is sense-making demand forecasting basically S&OP?
They overlap because both are cross-functional, but Yamaguchi puts more emphasis on a transparent base forecast, causal drivers, and demand-shaping action for new products.
IF PRESSED So I would say it is related to S&OP, but it is not exactly the same concept.
10What does AI add here?
AI can help identify demand drivers, estimate relationships, and make assumptions more visible, but the organization still needs a baseline and a way to evaluate whether AI added value.
IF PRESSED A proof of concept is not enough if the company cannot compare it with the previous forecasting process.
11What is FVA?
FVA means Forecast Value Added. It asks whether a step in the forecasting process actually improves the forecast compared with a benchmark or previous version.
IF PRESSED In this presentation, it matters because the company needs to know whether AI or a human adjustment really improved the result.
12What is Bias?
Bias is the systematic tendency to over-forecast or under-forecast.
IF PRESSED Unlike absolute error measures, the sign matters because it tells us the direction of the error.
13What does VUCA stand for?
Volatility, Uncertainty, Complexity, and Ambiguity.
IF PRESSED The idea is that the environment changes too much for managers to wait for perfect information before acting.
14What do you mean by causal assumptions?
They are assumptions about which factors drive demand and how they affect it — for example product attributes, promotion, channel, market conditions, or price.
IF PRESSED The article wants those assumptions to be visible instead of hidden inside judgment or a black-box model.
15What is demand shaping?
Demand shaping means using business levers such as marketing, pricing, promotion, channel, or supply decisions to influence demand after seeing the forecast.
IF PRESSED Forecasting provides the baseline; management action tries to change the outcome.
16Why did you include your onboarding SOP example?
Because it gave me a concrete analogy for the demand-library idea: turning scattered knowledge into something visible, reusable, and less dependent on one person’s memory.

It is mainly a practitioner and conceptual article, not a full empirical study with a complete sample, research design, and statistical validation.
IF PRESSED So I use its framework and observations carefully rather than treating every number as a universal law.
18What would you research next?
I would research how to separate forecast error from the effect of management interventions after the forecast changes decisions.
